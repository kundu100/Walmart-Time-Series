Walmart is an American multinational retail corporation that operates a chain of hypermarkets, discount department stores, and grocery stores in the United States.
It has been facing issues in managing inventory - to match the demand with respect to supply.
It has requested Intellipaat to come up with useful insights using their data and make prediction models to forecast the sales for 12 to 16 weeks.
It has provided last 3 years weekly sales data containing 6435 rows and 8 columns to enable data prediction.
